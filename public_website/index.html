Step 0 — Runs on your laptop

One docker compose up starts local LLMs and local transcription. Live meeting capture is BYO — point at your own self-hosted Vexa endpoint or bring a Recall.ai key. BYOK keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) work alongside as fallbacks.
