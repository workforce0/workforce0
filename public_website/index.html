Step 0 — Runs on your laptop

One docker compose up starts local LLMs and local transcription. Live meeting capture is BYO — point at your own self-hosted Vexa endpoint, or skip live capture and upload recordings manually. BYOK keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) work alongside as fallbacks.

Local LLMs

Ollama + Qwen 3.5 (8B/32B) and Mistral Small 3 (24B). Pick a tier in the wizard; it reads your hardware.

Local STT

faster-whisper-server — meeting transcripts never leave your network.

Self-hosted Vexa endpoint

Apache 2.0 meeting bot — joins Google Meet/Zoom/Teams. Run Vexa yourself and point VEXA_API_URL at it; audio stays on your infra.

Loop-guarded critique

Primary drafts
Reviewers critique in parallel
Primary revises if confidence < threshold

Termination guards

maxReviewRounds: hard cap on revise→critique cycles
confidenceThreshold: early exit when reviewer confidence is high
maxSteps: global cap across all rounds
parallel reviewers: no serial chaining, no compounding latency
